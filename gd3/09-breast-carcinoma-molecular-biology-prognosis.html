Molecular subtypes

  • Luminal A: ER+/PR+, HER2−, low grade, slow, best response to hormone therapy
  • Luminal B: ER+, higher grade/proliferation, may be HER2+ (BRCA2 cancers often luminal B)
  • HER2-enriched: ER−/PR−, HER2 3+ → chemo + trastuzumab
  • Basal-like (triple negative): ER−/PR−/HER2−, myoepithelial markers, young women, BRCA1, medullary pattern

Hereditary genes

  • BRCA1: breast + ovary, medullary/basal-like
  • BRCA2: male breast cancer, Fanconi anaemia (biallelic)
  • TP53: Li-Fraumeni · PTEN: Cowden (benign tumours, thyroid, endometrium) · CDH1: lobular + diffuse gastric · STK11: Peutz-Jeghers

Risk & prognosis

  • Protective: long breastfeeding, early menopause; risk: alcohol, late menopause, postmenopausal obesity
  • Major prognostic factors: distant metastasis (most important), lymph node status (number of positive axillary nodes best predicts survival), tumour size, inflammatory carcinoma, invasion
  • Minor: histologic type, grade, ER/PR & HER2, proliferation, aneuploidy (poor)
  • Sentinel node negative → spare axillary dissection; DCIS adequately treated is curative

Q24AMBOSS formativeClinical case

A 42-year-old woman with a breast lump has mammography showing a 6.5-mm mass with an irregular, spiculated border. Core needle biopsy shows infiltrating ductal carcinoma that is ERBB2 (HER2)-positive, ER-negative and PR-negative. Blood counts and liver tests are normal. What is the most appropriate next step?

Answer C: Breast-conserving surgery and sentinel lymph node biopsy

A small localised invasive carcinoma → breast-conserving surgery with sentinel node biopsy (then radiation ± trastuzumab for HER2+). Tamoxifen is useless in ER− tumours; PET/CT is for suspected metastases; sentinel biopsy replaces node FNA when nodes are not palpable.

Source: AMBOSS Team 224 · CBL Week 2 Session 1 · Q5

Q25AMBOSS formativeClinical case

A 56-year-old woman comes to the physician because she palpated a mass in her right breast during self-examination a week ago. Menarche was at the age of 14, and her last menstrual period was at the age of 51. Vital signs are within normal limits. Examination shows a nontender, firm, hard mass in the upper outer quadrant of the right breast. Mammography shows large, dense breasts, with a 1.7-cm mass in the right upper outer quadrant. The patient undergoes right upper outer quadrant lumpectomy with subsequent sentinel node biopsy, which reveals moderately differentiated invasive ductal carcinoma and micrometastasis to one axillary lymph node. There is no evidence of extranodal metastasis. The tumor tests positive for both estrogen and progesterone receptors and does not show human epidermal growth factor receptor 2 (ERBB2) over-expression. Flow-cytometry reveals aneuploid tumor cells. Which of the following factors has the greatest effect on this patient’s prognosis?

Answer D: Nodal status

Axillary lymph node involvement is one of the strongest predictors of breast cancer prognosis. The presence and extent of regional lymph node metastasis reflect the likelihood that the tumor has spread beyond the primary site.

Source: AMBOSS Team 224 · CBL Week 2 Session 1 · Q6

Q26AMBOSS formativeClinical case

A 34-year-old woman comes to the physician because of a rapidly growing lump in her right breast. Eight months ago, she underwent excision of a liposarcoma from the dorsum of her right forearm. Her brother was diagnosed with osteosarcoma of the maxilla at the age of 22 years. Her mother died at the age of 43 years from complications of acute myeloid leukemia. She has smoked a pack of cigarettes daily for 7 years. Examination shows a fixed, firm mass in the right breast with axillary lymphadenopathy. A biopsy of the mass confirms the diagnosis of invasive ductal carcinoma. Which of the following genetic principles is most likely responsible for this patient's condition?

Answer B: Loss of heterozygosity in tumor suppressor gene

Li-Fraumeni syndrome results from an inherited TP53 mutation; cancer develops when the remaining normal copy is lost or mutated (loss of heterozygosity).
