Dua for studying

اللَّهُمَّ انْفَعْنِي بِمَا عَلَّمْتَنِي، وَعَلِّمْنِي مَا يَنْفَعُنِي، وَزِدْنِي عِلْمًا
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ فَهْمَ النَّبِيِّينَ، وَحِفْظَ الْمُرْسَلِينَ وَالْمَلَائِكَةِ الْمُقَرَّبِينَ
Allāhumma-nfaʿnī bimā ʿallamtanī, wa ʿallimnī mā yanfaʿunī, wa zidnī ʿilmā.
Allāhumma innī as’aluka fahma-n-nabiyyīn, wa ḥifẓa-l-mursalīn wa-l-malā’ikati-l-muqarrabīn.
O Allah, make what You have taught me useful to me, teach me what will benefit me, and increase me in knowledge. O Allah, I ask You for the understanding of the prophets, and the memory of the messengers and the angels nearest to You.

Dua for something difficult

اللَّهُمَّ لَا سَهْلَ إِلَّا مَا جَعَلْتَهُ سَهْلًا، وَأَنْتَ تَجْعَلُ الْحَزْنَ إِذَا شِئْتَ سَهْلًا
Allāhumma lā sahla illā mā jaʿaltahu sahlā, wa anta tajʿalu-l-ḥazna idhā shi’ta sahlā.
O Allah, nothing is easy except what You make easy, and You make the difficult easy if You wish.
